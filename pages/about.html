Institute Overview

Bodhinex Institute is a proud initiative of the Bodhi Charitable Trust, dedicated to shaping brighter futures and empowering every learner across Chennai and beyond.

We are part of the Bodhinex Group of Chennai — an organization providing quality vocational education that bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world skills.
